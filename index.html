How searching works

Keywords finds lines containing all the words you type, in any order. Phrase finds the words together, in order. Regular expression works like grep -i on each line (JavaScript syntax).

Ignore diacritics lets siva find śiva and krsna find kṛṣṇa. It applies to regular expressions too. Whole words matches only complete space-separated words; remember that sandhi often joins words.

Regular expression examples: ^atha (lines beginning with atha), rasa(ḥ|m|sya), vāta.{0,20}pitta (pitta within 20 characters after vāta), āha\s*/+$ (lines ending in āha before the daṇḍa).

References like 12.3 are the DCS numbering (verse or sentence 12, line 3) at the end of each line in the source file. Click one to open that line on GitHub. You can bookmark or share any search: the address bar keeps it.
